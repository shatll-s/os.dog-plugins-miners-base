# RandomX third-party notices

No RandomX code distributed with XMRig remains in this plugin. The vendored tree
under `upstream/` - an x86-64 parity JIT and an AArch64 VM/dataset JIT - was
deleted once bzminer's own emitters replaced it on both architectures
(x86-64 September 2026, AArch64 September 23 2026).

What remains is adapted rather than copied, and is still acknowledged here: the
512-bit VAES lane-packing strategy in `rx/aes.cpp`, and the redundant-CFROUND
analysis, reciprocal calculation, and executable-code layout ideas in
`rx/jit_x86.cpp`.
